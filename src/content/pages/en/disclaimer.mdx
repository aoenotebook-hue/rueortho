## Nothing on this site is medical advice

- Everything here is general information. It is not a diagnosis, a consultation or a treatment plan for any individual, and it cannot replace being examined by and talking to a doctor.
- Reading or using this site does not create a doctor–patient relationship between you and the author.
- The same symptom can have many causes. A correct diagnosis needs a history, a physical examination and, sometimes, tests — carried out by a doctor who has actually seen you.
- Where medicines, exercises, procedures or operations are mentioned, this is general description only. It is not a recommendation to start or stop any treatment without speaking to the doctor looking after you.

## Emergencies

Go to an emergency department or call {site.emergencyNumber} (Thailand) immediately if you have: a serious injury, a limb that looks deformed or cannot bear weight, sudden numbness or weakness, a joint that is swollen, red and hot together with a fever, pain you cannot control, or loss of bladder or bowel control.
