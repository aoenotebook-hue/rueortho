รู้เรื่องกระดูกและข้อ (Easyortho) exists to help the general public understand common bone, joint and muscle problems, in language that is easy to read.

## Who this site is for

For people with symptoms who want to understand what is happening in their body, people who have just been diagnosed and want to know what comes next, and family members helping someone else.

It is written for a reader on a phone with no medical background. Every medical term is explained in plain language the first time it appears.

## What this site does not do

- It does not give personal medical advice and cannot diagnose you.
- It does not take appointments, give individual consultations, or answer questions about your symptoms by e-mail.
- It carries no advertising and recommends no hospital, clinic, product or supplement.

The aim is to help you understand your own condition and talk to your doctor with more confidence — not to replace your doctor.

## How the articles are written

Every article is written and reviewed by an orthopaedic surgeon. The author, the reviewer, the publication date and the last review date appear at the top of each article, with references at the end. Articles are re-reviewed at least every 24 months.

Read more in the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy).
