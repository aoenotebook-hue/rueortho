This page explains how the articles on รู้เรื่องกระดูกและข้อ (Easyortho) are written, reviewed and kept up to date, so that you can judge how far to trust them.

## Purpose

To help the general public understand common bone, joint and muscle problems in plain language that reflects current medical evidence, and to help you have better conversations with your own doctor. The site is not intended to help you diagnose or treat yourself.

## Who writes and reviews

Every article is written and reviewed by a specialist orthopaedic surgeon. The author's name appears at the end of each article, and the author reviews his own work.

## Sources

Content is based on peer-reviewed textbooks and research, clinical practice guidelines from Thai and international professional bodies (for example the Royal College of Orthopaedic Surgeons of Thailand, the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons and NICE), and the author's clinical experience. References are listed at the end of every article. We avoid sources with commercial interests.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please write to {site.contactEmail}. We will check and correct it promptly; significant corrections are noted in the article with a date.

## Independence

The site has no sponsors and no advertising, and the author receives no payment from any pharmaceutical, device or healthcare company for writing it.
