This page explains how the articles on รู้เรื่องกระดูกและข้อ (Easyortho) are written, reviewed and kept up to date, so that you can judge how far to trust them.

## Purpose

To help the general public understand common bone, joint and muscle problems in plain language that reflects current medical evidence, and to help you have better conversations with your own doctor. The site is not intended to help you diagnose or treat yourself.

## Who writes and reviews

Every article is written and reviewed by a specialist orthopaedic surgeon. The author, the reviewer, the publication date and the date of the last review are shown at the top of each article. The author's background is on the "About the author" page.

## Sources

Content is based on peer-reviewed textbooks and research, clinical practice guidelines from Thai and international professional bodies (for example the Royal College of Orthopaedic Surgeons of Thailand, the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons and NICE), and the author's clinical experience. References are listed at the end of every article. We avoid sources with commercial interests.

## How an article is produced

1. A standard structure is applied (what it is, symptoms, causes, when to see a doctor, diagnosis, treatment, recovery, prevention, questions to ask your doctor).
2. The text is drafted from the author's teaching material and the sources above.
3. It is checked for medical accuracy, safety (the warning signs that need a doctor) and readability.
4. It is published with its dates, and corrected when readers give feedback.

## Use of artificial intelligence

We use AI tools to help draft text, translate between Thai and English and check spelling. Every medical sentence is checked, edited and approved by the author before publication. Responsibility for the content rests with the author, not the tool.

## Review cycle

Every article is re-reviewed at least every 24 months, or sooner when a new guideline or important evidence changes practice. An article past its review date shows a notice to readers.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please write to {site.contactEmail}. We will check and correct it promptly; significant corrections are noted in the article with a date.

## Independence

The site has no sponsors and no advertising, and the author receives no payment from any pharmaceutical, device or healthcare company for writing it.
