## What we collect and why

### 1. Visit statistics (anonymous)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect data that identifies you. It tells us, in aggregate, how many people visit, which pages are read, the country of origin, and the device and browser type. We use this to improve the content. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interests (Section 24(5)).

### 2. E-mails you send us

If you contact us by e-mail we keep your name, e-mail address and message solely to reply, and delete them within 12 months of the last contact unless the law requires otherwise. Legal basis: legitimate interests and responding to your request. Please do not send personal health information, test results or medical images by e-mail — we cannot give individual advice.

### 3. Technical logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP address and access time for security and operation of the service.

## Third-party services used on this site

- Hosting: Vercel Inc. (United States)
- Analytics: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States)
- Fonts: Google Fonts — when a page loads, your browser requests font files from Google's servers, which may see your IP address.
- Video: YouTube videos load only after you press play; from then on Google's privacy policy applies.

Using these services means some technical data may be transferred outside Thailand. All are major providers with internationally recognised data-protection safeguards.

## What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, do not run behavioural advertising, and do not build user profiles.

## Your rights under the PDPA

You may ask to access, receive a copy of, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to have it transferred, and you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC). To exercise a right, contact {site.contactEmail}; we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has sent us information, let us know and we will delete it.
