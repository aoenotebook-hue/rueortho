## What we can and cannot answer

We are glad to receive corrections, suggestions and PDPA requests.

We **cannot give individual medical advice by e-mail** — no diagnosis, no interpretation of test results, no second opinions, and no treatment recommendations for your symptoms. A correct diagnosis needs a history and a physical examination by a doctor who has actually seen you.

For the same reason, **please do not send personal health information, test results, X-ray films or medical images by e-mail.** If you do, we will not use them and will delete them.

We do not take appointments and cannot recommend a particular doctor or hospital.

## Emergencies

If you have urgent symptoms — a serious injury, a limb that looks deformed or cannot bear weight, sudden numbness or weakness, a joint that is swollen, red and hot with a fever, or loss of bladder or bowel control — **do not wait for an e-mail reply.** Go to the nearest doctor or emergency department immediately, or call {site.emergencyNumber} in Thailand.
